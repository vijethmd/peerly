    How it works

    Click, share, talk.

    1. 1

      Start a meeting

      One click gives you a private link. There’s nothing to sign up for and nothing to download.

    2. 2

      Share the link

      Send it to up to seven other people. They join from any modern browser, on a laptop or a phone.

    3. 3

      Talk

      Video, captions and chat go straight between you. Hang up and get the notes.

    Features

    Everything a meeting needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

    01

    Private by design

    Audio and video go straight between participants, end-to-end encrypted by WebRTC. The server never sees them.

    02

    AI meeting notes

    Live captions, a searchable transcript, a “catch me up” recap, and notes with decisions and action items when you hang up.

    03

    Backgrounds and blur

    Blur your room or pick a virtual background. It all runs on your device, so your camera feed isn’t uploaded anywhere.

    04

    Reactions and chat

    Send reactions everyone sees, raise your hand, and message the whole meeting or one person privately.

    05

    Stays connected

    Calls keep running through Wi-Fi drops, page reloads and server updates, and reconnect on their own.

    06

    Host controls

    Ask-to-join waiting room, mute, spotlight, presenting approval, and end the meeting for everyone.
